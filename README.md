# is401-datamodel_and_backend
APP SUMMARY: 5-8 sentences on the problem your product solves, who experiences it, and what the app does.
Our product solves the issue of organizing and receiving notifications 

BYU students often struggle to keep track of assignments and deadlines because their coursework can be spread across multiple classes, platforms, and tools. Week by week, professors are adding and updating assignments, requiring students to repeatedly check different locations to understand what is due and when it is due, which leads to a lot of stress and confusion. There is not one centralized dashboard to clearly show us upcoming deadlines. Also, there is no platform to directly notify our computers and cellphones with due dates and reminders for our assignments. As a result, students can overlook assignments, miss deadlines, and waste a lot of time checking different locations daily to see what they need to complete for school. BYU students need a centralized way to view, prioritize, and receive reminders about upcoming assignments so they can stay organized, focused, and efficiently learn in a simpler way.
