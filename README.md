# is401-datamodel_and_backend
APP SUMMARY: 5-8 sentences on the problem your product solves, who experiences it, and what the app does.
Our product solves the issue of organizing and receiving notifications 

BYU students often struggle to keep track of assignments and deadlines because their coursework can be spread across multiple classes, platforms, and tools. Week by week, professors are adding and updating assignments, requiring students to repeatedly check different locations to understand what is due and when it is due, which leads to a lot of stress and confusion. There is not one centralized dashboard to clearly show upcoming deadlines, and no platform to directly notify computers or cellphones with due dates and reminders for assignments. This app is designed to integrate Canvas and Learning Suite assignments into one place automatically and allow BYU Students to see all their assignments together and set up notifications to be better alerted.
